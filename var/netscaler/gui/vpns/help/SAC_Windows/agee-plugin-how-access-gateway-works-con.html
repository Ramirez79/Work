How Access Gateway Works

To log on to your internal enterprise network, you need to log on to Access Gateway. To do this, you use the Access Gateway Plug-in. If you are trying to log on to your internal network, install the plug-in on your computer and then log on.

The Access Gateway Plug-in is installed on your computer when you log on for the first time. After the plug-in is installed, you can log on using the icon on your desktop or you can connect using a Web portal page.

When the Access Gateway Plug-in is downloaded to your computer and a connection is made, Access Gateway creates a secure channel of communication between your computer and Access Gateway, and allows you to access resources in the internal network that you are authorized to use.

Your system administrator can also configure the Access Gateway Plug-in to ensure that certain personal firewalls and antivirus applications are running on your computer.
