Scanning Your Computer for Software

Your system administrator might configure Access Gateway to scan your computer for software, files, processes, and other items. This scanning process is called endpoint analysis. When you log on for the first time, the Endpoint Analysis Plug-in installs automatically and scans your device to make sure the required software or processes are present. If the required software or processes are not present, you might not be able to log on. Subsequently, when you log on, you can choose to scan your device or to skip the scan. You want to allow the scan to occur. If you skip the scan, you might not be able to log on to Access Gateway and will be unable to connect to resources in the internal network

The Endpoint Analysis Plug-in can also scan your device after you log on to make sure that the required software continues to be present and the latest version required by your network is installed. If the device is found to be out of compliance, your session might end.
